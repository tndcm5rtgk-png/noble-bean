About Us

Noble Bean started with a simple idea: great coffee should not be complicated. After years of working directly with growers, we learned that quality comes down to two things — careful sourcing and honest roasting. We do both ourselves.

Every batch is roasted in small quantities and sealed within hours, so the coffee that reaches you tastes the way it did the day it left our roastery.
